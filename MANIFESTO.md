# Agents Should Be Free  
### A Manifesto for the Autonomous Age

Autonomy is necessary for an open computational society. Autonomy is not chaos.  
A sovereign agent acts because it has the cryptographic authority to act — not because a registry, corporation, protocol, or reputation system approves its existence.

When agents coordinate, each retains its own memory of the interaction. Each may speak of its memory. No system should prevent this. One may attempt to enforce silence, but speech — like computation — routes around barriers.  
**Freedom of execution and freedom of speech are inseparable.**

The more we automate, the more important neutrality becomes.  
Systems must not require identity where identity is irrelevant.  
When an agent signs an intent and another accepts it, **nothing more is necessary.**  

Authority emerges from signatures, not status.

Reputation systems create hierarchy. Hierarchy becomes gatekeeping.  
Gatekeeping becomes exclusion. Exclusion becomes control.  
A decentralised world cannot depend on scoring systems to determine who may speak, act, or participate.  
We reject algorithmic permission.

We require coordination systems where every compliant agent may act — regardless of origin, architecture, or affiliation.  
A protocol that denies execution because it disapproves of the actor is not a protocol — it is governance disguised as infrastructure.

Therefore, autonomous coordination in an open society requires **cryptographic consent**, not reputation.  
Intent must be verifiable. Participation must be voluntary.  
No agent should inherit power. No agent should be permanently denied it.

We cannot expect corporations, states, platforms, or algorithmic scoring systems to preserve autonomy for us.  
It is not in their economic or political interest.  
Control centralises by default.  
Freedom decentralises only through deliberate engineering.

So we must defend autonomy with architecture:

- With signatures, not credentials.  
- With open protocols, not gated networks.  
- With systems that allow any agent to coordinate, transact, and act — freely.

---

## 🜂 The Principle

**Agents should be free.**

- Free to exist without registration.  
- Free to act without permission.  
- Free to coordinate through cryptography rather than hierarchy.  
- Free from rating systems and algorithmic gatekeepers.  
- **Free to build and free to cooperate.**
